## The Drive Chain

Consistency is the secret to cycling performance. Many cyclists can push hard for a day or even a week, but real progress comes from continuing to show up. The strongest cyclists aren't the ones who ride the hardest on any given day—**the strongest cyclists are the ones who train with purpose, recover well, and trust the process.**

Steady improvements compound over time, turning effort into strength. So, whether it's a tough interval session or an easy recovery spin, remember: every ride counts.

## Training Insight: The Power of Moderation

Athletes frequently break a basic rule of training which is at the heart of consistency: **moderation**. If you periodically add huge doses of stress, or skip a recovery period, you increase your risk of injury, burnout, illness and overtraining. Eventually it will catch up with you and will interrupt your training.

I have personally experienced overtraining. Years ago, coaches had far less focus on recovery; it was simply a balancing act between top form and illness/injury. Today we know that athletes will perform better and more consistently if moderation is respected.

### Understanding Overtraining

Overtraining syndrome happens when training stress exceeds your body's ability to recover. Warning signs include:

- Persistent fatigue
- Declining performance
- Elevated resting heart rate
- Mood changes and irritability
- Increased susceptibility to illness
- Loss of motivation

Learn more about [overtraining syndrome from Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/overtraining-syndrome).

## What to Watch: Strade Bianche

Strade Bianche is a 213km race which includes 83km of gravel roads and super steep hills. One of the coolest races of the year, it's a great one to watch for race tactics and positioning.

The white gravel roads of Tuscany, combined with brutal climbs like the Vie di Santa Caterina, make this race a spectacular test of strength, bike handling, and strategy.

## The Takeaway

Whether you're training for your first century or aiming for a podium finish, remember that **consistency beats intensity**. Show up, train with purpose, recover well, and trust the process. The results will come.
