## Science: Heat Training for Cyclists

Racing and training in the heat isn't just about surviving—**it's about adapting.** Research shows that structured heat exposure leads to physiological changes:

### The Adaptations

**Bigger blood volume**  
More plasma and red blood cells to deliver oxygen efficiently.

**Better thermoregulation**  
Earlier sweating, lower core temp at the same workload.

**Improved VO₂max**  
Heat training can raise both your lactate threshold and all-out power, even in cooler conditions.

**Performance carry-over**  
Adaptations gained in the heat benefit endurance performance year-round.

### Best Practices

**Aim for consistent exposure**  
5x per week for 3–5 weeks

**Use both active and passive methods:**
- Active: Rides in hot conditions
- Passive: Sauna, hot baths (15-30 minutes post-ride)

**Stay on top of hydration and electrolytes**  
Drink before you're thirsty. Replace sodium losses.

**Watch recovery**  
Heat stress is real stress! Monitor resting heart rate and sleep quality.

### Sample Heat Training Protocol

**Week 1-2:** Acclimation
- 60-90 min rides in heat
- Zone 2 intensity
- Focus on hydration practice

**Week 3-4:** Adaptation
- 90-120 min rides
- Include tempo efforts
- Add post-ride sauna (15-20 min)

**Week 5+:** Maintenance
- 2-3x per week heat exposure
- Normal training intensity
- Continue electrolyte focus
