## Training Insight: Hydration in the Heat

**Texas is HOT!** Drinking to thirst may be enough in the winter, but as the weather heats up we need to proactively plan hydration. Cyclists should drink fluids regularly before, during, and after riding, rather than waiting until you feel thirsty.

I recently started using more electrolytes when riding over 2 hours, and found that by increasing electrolytes my feeling later in the ride is improved. Staying hydrated is an important part of cycling performance.

### Hydration Guidelines for Cyclists

- **Before riding:** Start hydrated - drink 16-20 oz of water 2-3 hours before your ride
- **During riding:** Aim for 20-30 oz per hour, adjusting for temperature and intensity
- **After riding:** Rehydrate with electrolyte-rich fluids to replace what you've lost
- **For rides over 2 hours:** Add electrolytes to maintain sodium, potassium, and magnesium levels

## Athlete Spotlight: Johan Museeuw - The Lion of Flanders

![Johan Museeuw racing Paris-Roubaix in 2000](/assets/images/journal/riders/johan-museeuw.jpg)
*Photo: [Matheus Katharus](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Museeuw_Roubaix_2000.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)*

This week, let's look at Johan Museeuw, one of the greatest Classics riders of all time. Known as **"The Lion of Flanders,"** Museeuw dominated races like Paris-Roubaix and the Tour of Flanders in the '90s. His grit and resilience, especially after a devastating knee injury, define what it means to be a true competitor.

Museeuw once said:

*"To win in the cobbled classics, you need power, skill, and the ability to suffer. But most importantly, you need the mindset to keep pushing when everything hurts."*

### Museeuw's Training Approach

Here's a breakdown of his legendary training methodology:

#### High Volume, Long Rides
Museeuw regularly rode extensive distances, often around 300km daily, focusing on building endurance and strength.

#### Coastal Training
He trained on the flat coastal plain around Gistel, Belgium, where strong winds provided a unique challenge.

#### Cobblestone Specificity
His training included riding on cobblestones, which is crucial for success in the Spring Classics.

#### No Stopping During Training
He would only stop during training if he needed food or drink, emphasizing the importance of maintaining momentum.

#### Emphasis on Spring Classics
His training was geared towards the Spring Classics, focusing on the ability to perform well over long distances.

#### "Killer Instinct"
Museeuw emphasized the importance of not only being a strong rider but also having a "killer instinct" to win races.

## Quick Tip: Mid-Ride Caffeine Boost

Feeling sluggish mid-ride? Try a quick caffeine boost (**~1.5 mg per kg of body weight**) to sharpen focus and increase endurance.

For a 70kg (154 lb) rider, that's about 105mg of caffeine - roughly equivalent to:
- 1 caffeine gel
- 1 espresso shot
- Half an energy drink

Timing matters: consume 30-60 minutes before you need the performance boost.
