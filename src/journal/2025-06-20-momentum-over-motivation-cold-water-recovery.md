## Start Strong: It's Not Motivation, It's Momentum

Motivation is fleeting, especially after a tough week or a busy summer. Once you're out there pedaling, that first mile leads to the next.

**Momentum is motivation in motion.**

Just show up. Your legs will handle the rest.

### The Psychology of Getting Started

The hardest part of any ride isn't the climb or the intervals—it's **getting out the door.** Once you clip in and start turning the pedals, something shifts. The resistance fades. The doubt quiets. You remember why you ride.

This is momentum at work.

**The Takeaway:** Don't wait to feel motivated. Create momentum by starting small. Just get on the bike. The rest will follow.

## Science Spotlight: Cold Water Immersion — Hype or Help?

A recent study from the *Journal of Sports Sciences* (2024) compared **cold water immersion (CWI)** vs. **active recovery** after high-intensity cycling.

### Key Takeaway

CWI reduced **perceived soreness by 20%** but showed **no significant performance gains** the next day compared to light spinning.

### What This Means for Cyclists

**Active recovery (easy spinning) is still king** for physiological recovery:
- Increases blood flow to muscles
- Clears metabolic waste (lactate, etc.)
- Promotes adaptation without adding fatigue

**Cold water immersion works for perception:**
- Makes you *feel* better mentally
- Reduces inflammation and soreness
- Can be a psychological reset after hard efforts

### Coach's Tip

**Don't skip that recovery spin**—it may do more than the ice bath. But for a **psychological reset**, cold water can still work wonders.

Use CWI strategically:
- After multi-day stage races
- During heavy training blocks
- When mental fatigue is high

**Source:** *J Sports Sci*, 2024;42(3):312-320

## Athlete Spotlight: Tim Merlier – The Belgian Bullet

![Tim Merlier at the 2025 Tour de France](/assets/images/journal/riders/tim-merlier.jpg)
*Photo: [Renhour48](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tour_de_France_2025_-_Tim_Merlier_N24.jpg), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)*

When Tim Merlier lines up, you know a sprint showdown is coming. The **Belgian national champion** and **current European champion** has made his mark with explosive power.

### Fast Facts

**Top Speed:** Clocked over **75 kph** in bunch sprints

**Training Focus:**
- Sprint simulation
- Torque work (big gear efforts)
- Execution under fatigue

**Mindset:**  
*"Train with intention. Race with instinct."*

### What Makes Merlier Dangerous

Whether on cobbles or asphalt, Merlier reminds us: **sprinting isn't chaos—it's calculated.**

He doesn't just have raw power. He has:
- **Positioning awareness** (knows where to be in the final 500m)
- **Timing** (launches at the perfect moment)
- **Bike handling** (can sprint through technical finishes)
- **Mental toughness** (thrives under pressure)

### Merlier's Training Philosophy

*"You don't need to train 30 hours a week to go fast. You do need to train with purpose."*  
— Tim Merlier

This is a critical lesson for all cyclists:

**More isn't always better. Better is better.**

- Quality intervals > junk miles
- Intentional recovery > riding tired every day
- Focused goals > scattered training

### Sprint Training Lessons from Merlier

Even if you're not a sprinter, Merlier's approach applies:

**1. Simulate race conditions in training**  
Don't just do intervals—practice the chaos of real racing.

**2. Build torque, not just cadence**  
Big gear efforts develop raw power and strength.

**3. Train under fatigue**  
Sprints at the end of long rides teach your body to perform when tired.

**4. Execute with confidence**  
Merlier doesn't hesitate. When the moment comes, he commits 100%.

## Coach's Corner: Weekly Training Reminders

**Keep your Z2 honest**  
Constant pressure on the pedals. No coasting. No surging. Steady aerobic work builds the foundation.

**Threshold is meant to feel hard**  
That pain is where the gains live. If FTP intervals feel comfortable, you're not at threshold.

**Off-bike matters**  
Prioritize:
- **Sleep** (7-9 hours minimum)
- **Carbs** (fuel your training, don't fear carbs)
- **Stretching/mobility** (especially your back and hips)

## The Momentum Mindset

You don't need to feel motivated every day. You just need to **start moving.**

The first pedal stroke leads to the second. The second leads to the third. Before you know it, you're 10 miles in and feeling strong.

**Action beats intention every time.**
